### `prism/` — a learning algorithm, unrelated to the test above

A mixture of local linear laws whose regions are placed by the residuals of the
laws they carry, rather than by density or by greedy splitting. Closed-form
steps throughout, and it runs unchanged on NumPy or CuPy. See
[`prism/README.md`](prism/README.md); benchmarks in `benchmarks/bench_prism.py`,
tests in `tests/test_prism.py`.
